About DolarForge

Independent, affiliate-supported product research focused on practical buyer questions, transparent limitations, and verifiable sources.

Independent affiliate-supported publishing

DolarForge publishes practical product guides for online shoppers. Some outbound links are affiliate links, meaning we may receive a commission without increasing the buyer’s price.

We aim to disclose affiliate relationships, avoid guaranteed outcomes, identify limitations, and encourage readers to verify current details directly with the seller.

How our reviews are prepared

  • We review the seller's current presentation, label information when available, refund terms, and ClickBank marketplace context.
  • For health-related topics, we compare marketing claims with sources such as government guidance and peer-reviewed research.
  • We distinguish commercial signals—such as marketplace traction—from evidence that a finished product produces a health outcome.
  • We record important limitations and avoid describing a supplement as a replacement for professional care.
  • We update a review date only when the page receives a meaningful content or evidence update.

Our full scoring factors are explained on the methodology page.

What DolarForge does not claim

DolarForge is not a healthcare provider, laboratory, product manufacturer, or seller. Unless a review explicitly states and documents otherwise, we have not personally used or laboratory-tested the product. We do not create customer testimonials or guarantee results.

Corrections and current information

Offers, formulas, prices, shipping, and refund conditions can change. Readers should verify live terms before buying. If you find a factual error or outdated link, use our contact page so we can review it.
